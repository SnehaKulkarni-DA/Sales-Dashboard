📈 Business Growth Monitor Dashboard Overview

The Business Growth Monitor is an interactive dashboard designed to track and analyze key business performance indicators. It provides insights into revenue, orders, payment methods, and product categories, enabling stakeholders to monitor growth and make data-driven decisions.

✨ Features
Key Metrics

💰 Total Revenue: ₹45

⏱️ Average Delivery Days: 45.31K

📦 Total Orders: 1

💳 Payment Method Revenue: ₹45

Visual Insights

📊 Avg Delivery Days by Category: Breakdown by product category (e.g., Accessories).

🥧 Revenue by Payment Method: Donut chart showing 100% revenue via Credit Card.

📦 Orders by Product Name: Bar chart highlighting Wireless Mouse as the top product.

🎯 Revenue vs Last Month Sales: Gauge chart comparing current revenue (₹45) against target (₹90).

Interactive Filters

Category (Accessories, Technology, Office Supplies, etc.)

Payment Method (COD, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI)

Product Name (Keyboard, Laptop, Smartphone, USB Cable, Wireless Mouse, etc.)

📌 Insights

Credit Card is the dominant payment method (100% of revenue).

Wireless Mouse is the only product ordered in the dataset.

Current revenue is at 50% of the monthly target (₹45 out of ₹90).
